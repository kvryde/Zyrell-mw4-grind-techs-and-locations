MW4 Recipe Book

DMZ crafting, blueprints and grind routes

Grind playbook
  • Put recipes and manuals in a backpack slot that is protected, so they survive a failed exfil.
  • Open the 3D Printer in the FOB and track the ingredients you need. Loot spawns by location type, so head to places that match the item’s theme.
  • Complete DMZ Orders. Some recipes, like the Large Backpack, are order rewards.
  • Craft each owned weapon blueprint once for free at the Gunsmith, then run with it.
  • Do loot-only runs first to stock ingredients, then fight-focused runs once you’re geared up.

Starter entries come from public MW4 launch coverage. Ingredient lists, drop spots and exact blueprint sources can change with patches, so use Edit to add what you find in-game. Everything saves in your own browser.
